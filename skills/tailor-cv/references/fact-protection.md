# Candidate fact protection

The baseline and private profile remain the factual authority. Public Skill files contain no candidate history.

Before delivery, compare the target with the baseline and classify changes:

- presentation: order, wording, emphasis, or layout changed without changing meaning;
- omission: supported material was removed or compressed;
- factual correction: a fact changed and requires explicit confirmation;
- new claim: material not present in the approved sources and therefore blocked until confirmed.

For each material proposal, record its section, classification, rationale, supporting evidence IDs, and decision (`accepted`, `rejected`, or `pending`). A document is ready only when no proposal is pending. Keep this review record beside the private source, not inside the final CV.

Check names, contact fields, organizations, role titles, dates, credentials, grades, metrics, tools, languages, publications, links, and file metadata. Whitespace-only source changes are not factual changes.

Keep source URLs and access dates in a private sidecar or handoff, not in the final CV. Report when automated comparison or visual rendering is unavailable.
