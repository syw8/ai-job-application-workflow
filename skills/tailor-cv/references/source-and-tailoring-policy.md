# CV source and tailoring policy

## Source order

1. Primary vacancy and application instructions.
2. Employer or institution career guidance.
3. Relevant government employment service, official EU service, or national public career service.
4. University career service or clearly identified specialist authority.

Record publisher, URL, access date, and the applicable rule. Recheck the sources for each target market; do not assume one country's convention applies everywhere.

Useful starting authorities include:

- Bundesagentur für Arbeit: <https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/bewerbungstraining/bewerbung-schreiben>
- Europass CV guidance: <https://europass.europa.eu/en/create-europass-cv>

## Allowed tailoring

Subject to configuration and user review, tailoring may:

- rewrite the professional summary using supported facts;
- reorder sections and bullets;
- expand relevant duties and compress less relevant detail;
- select relevant projects, publications, coursework, or interests;
- align terminology with the vacancy when the meaning remains true;
- reorganize skills and languages without changing proficiency;
- adjust layout to meet a stated page or format requirement.

Tailoring must not change the underlying employer, institution, title, date, credential, grade, result, authorship, skill level, or contact fact.
