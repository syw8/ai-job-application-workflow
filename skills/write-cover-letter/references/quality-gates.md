# Cover-letter quality gates

## Research

- The primary vacancy was checked or its absence was disclosed.
- Relevant official guidance has a publisher, URL, and access date.
- Employer-specific instructions take priority over generic advice.

## Content

- Every candidate claim is supported by private evidence or explicit confirmation.
- Organization, role, requisition, salutation, location, and deadline are consistent with the source.
- The letter explains fit through examples rather than copying the CV or vacancy.
- Tone and spelling convention fit the target language and market.
- Required salary, availability, or reference details are included only when requested and confirmed.

## File

- The source baseline, if any, was not overwritten.
- The draft has a vacancy-specific version ID and no pending change decision.
- Export or compilation completed without fatal errors.
- Page target, margins, wrapping, links, and unexpected blank pages were checked when rendering is available.
- Private paths and source notes are not embedded in the final letter.

Report every skipped check.
