# Review checkpoint

Before continuing, the fictional candidate would confirm:

- the primary vacancy is still available and its requisition, location, deadline, and required files are correct;
- the guidance sources apply to this market and do not conflict with employer instructions;
- every item in the requirement-to-evidence map is accurate;
- every proposed change has an evidence link and an explicit decision;
- the CV's omissions and reordered material remain fair and understandable;
- the cover letter sounds like the candidate and contains no unsupported claim;
- the application record references the exact reviewed CV and cover-letter version IDs;
- the application record remains `planned` until direct submission evidence exists.
